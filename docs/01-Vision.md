# Executive Summary

Oxy AI Readiness is a premium commercial WordPress plugin that prepares websites for the next generation of Artificial Intelligence systems.

Unlike traditional SEO plugins that optimize websites for search engines, Oxy AI Readiness optimizes websites for AI Search, AI Agents, Large Language Models (LLMs), autonomous assistants, and future AI discovery standards.

The plugin provides automated AI readiness analysis, AI-specific optimizations, and one-click fixes that allow any WordPress website to become fully discoverable and compatible with modern AI ecosystems.

It is designed to become the industry standard for AI Optimization on WordPress.
