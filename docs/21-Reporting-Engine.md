# Reporting Engine

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Reporting Engine transforms technical data into meaningful reports for developers, agencies, business owners and enterprise teams.

Reports should not simply display data.

They should explain the current AI readiness of the website, identify risks, measure improvements and provide actionable recommendations.

---

# Vision

Every report should answer four questions.

Where are we?

What changed?

What should we improve?

What business impact does it have?

# Success Criteria

Reports should be understandable by both technical and non-technical users.

Every report must clearly communicate AI readiness, business impact, technical status and recommended next actions.

The Reporting Engine should become the central business intelligence layer of Oxy AI Readiness.
